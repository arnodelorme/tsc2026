    Mission alignment, not just visibility

    Why TSC 2026 is IONS's conference

    For four decades IONS has championed rigorous, open inquiry into consciousness and non-local phenomena. TSC 2026 advances that exact frontier — and the people in the room are precisely the IONS community.

    A shared scientific mission

    TSC 2026 advances rigorous, open inquiry into consciousness — the precise frontier IONS has championed for over four decades.

    IONS is already at the center

    IONS researchers and fellows are featured among the plenary speakers and the workshop leaders.

    Our exact community

    Scientists, clinicians, contemplatives, and supporters who care about noetic and non-local phenomena are precisely who attends.

    A leadership role

    With IONS President Thomas Brophy and IONS researcher Arnaud Delorme serving as conference co-chairs, the Institute helps shape TSC 2026 as it establishes its new San Diego home.

    Outside of IONS's own conferences, this is the largest gathering of IONS scientists and fellows ever assembled — on one stage, in one week.
