## Step 1: Identity Resolution

Gino Yu received his B.S. and Ph.D. from the University of California, Berkeley (1987 and 1993 respectively). After completing his doctorate, he taught at the University of Southern California where he helped establish multimedia initiatives including the Integrated Media Systems Center. From 1995 to 1997 he taught at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where he helped establish the Center for Enhanced Learning Technologies.

In 1999, Yu established the Multimedia Innovation Centre at Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) and became Associate Professor and Director of Digital Entertainment and Game Development in the School of Design. He co-founded the PolyU M-Lab, a commercially oriented digital entertainment laboratory. He has over 80 publications in academic conferences and journals.

Yu's research interests shifted over time from design automation and computer animation to consciousness studies. He founded the Asia Consciousness Festival and **hosted the 2009 edition of the Toward a Science of Consciousness (TSC) conference** — the direct predecessor of Consciousness Science 2026. He has spoken at TED, the Cannes Lion Festival, and the World Knowledge Forum.

He is a contributor to Closer to Truth and has written for HuffPost. He has appeared on multiple podcasts discussing consciousness and awakening.

Public profiles: personal website (ginoyu.com), ResearchGate, Google Scholar, X (@phusikoi), Closer to Truth, HuffPost

## Step 3: Association with Known High-Risk Figures

### **CRITICAL FINDING: Extensive documented relationship with Jeffrey Epstein (2015–2019)**

The DOJ Epstein files released February 3, 2026, contain extensive documentation of a sustained relationship between Gino Yu and Jeffrey Epstein. A search for "Gino Yu" on the DOJ's Epstein Library portal yields over 1,000 results. Hong Kong media outlet HK01 conducted a detailed investigation of the archive.

#### Scale of the relationship:

- **At least 548 emails** exchanged between Yu and Epstein from their first meeting in 2015 until Epstein's arrest in 2019.
- The pair communicated frequently, met privately, and attended social events together.
- Yu called Epstein his **"benefactor"** (per HK01 reporting).

#### Origin of the relationship:

Around 2010, AI researcher Ben Goertzel worked with Yu at PolyU on OpenCog, an AI framework. Goertzel approached Epstein for private funding that reportedly grew from $10,000 to $100,000. By 2013, Goertzel had introduced Yu to Epstein as a well-connected figure in Hong Kong who could bring "smart and interesting people" into Epstein's orbit. Yu and Epstein first met in person in 2015.

#### Key documented communications:

1. **"Is your island out of commission?" (October 2017):** Yu emailed Epstein asking about the availability of his island. Epstein replied that it was "destroyed" and that "the US will not allow travel ... so it's a no go." It is unclear whether Yu was referring to Little Saint James, Epstein's private island used as a hub for sex trafficking.

2. **The "stage 3" email (January 2018) — DEEPLY CONCERNING:** Yu wrote to Epstein describing a woman as *"a classic 'stage 3' in the model. Outlier who is intelligent, was traumatised when she was young (sexual abuse), and now gradually coming into her own... Starting to develop special abilities."* This email, in which Yu appears to categorize an abuse survivor using a developmental model and offer to connect her with Epstein, has been widely described as deeply disturbing. The email also referenced a London group using Iboga (a psychedelic) under "ALTERNATE PARADIGMS."

3. **VR headset for "your girls":** Yu wrote about getting Epstein and "your girls" a VR headset "to play with," with the cost framed as "ideally funded" out of "the 100K."

4. **Staying at Epstein's residence (2015):** Yu contacted Epstein's assistant to ask about available rooms at Epstein's residence.

5. **The "confessional" email (2018):** Yu sent Epstein a message musing about his own neurology, declaring "We're not crazy. We are ... geniuses." Epstein replied curtly.

6. **Arranging meetings between Epstein and others:** In 2017, Yu introduced an American female author to Epstein. After their meetings at Epstein's residence, Yu was copied on an email indicating he received fresh funding. In early 2019, German scientist Joscha Bach referenced Yu in an email with Epstein's assistant, saying Yu wanted an unnamed woman to join him at Davos and later travel to Zurich and Marrakech — her identity is redacted.

7. **Connection to Stuart Hameroff:** A 2016 email chain between Hameroff and Yu indicates that Hameroff stayed at one of Epstein's apartments in New York for multiple days around Halloween 2016, arranged by Yu. After the visit, Hameroff called the night "memorable." Yu also wanted James Tagg of the Penrose Institute to attend the meeting with Epstein and Hameroff.
