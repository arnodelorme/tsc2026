## Step 1: Identity Resolution

Eugene L. Jhong is a retired American software developer and philanthropist. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science from Stanford University (1995-1997). He worked at mySimon Inc. and subsequently at Google, where he was part of the Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) team. He holds patents assigned to Google Inc. related to automatic repair and replacement of computing machines. He left Google in 2006 to care for his mother.

Since retirement, Jhong has become a significant philanthropist focused on consciousness studies, psychedelics research, astrobiology, and frontier science. He is also listed as an angel investor on Wellfound (formerly AngelList). He has two sons and is based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

His personal interests include golf, jiu jitsu, meditation, breathing, and consciousness studies. He became interested in consciousness research after hearing Stuart Hameroff present on the Orch OR theory at the BrainMind Summit at Stanford.

Public profiles: LinkedIn, Medium (ejhong), Wellfound
