## Step 1: Identity Resolution

Cyan Banister (née Callihan; born 1977) is an American angel investor and entrepreneur. She grew up in Arizona and experienced homelessness at age 15, dropping out of high school. She is self-taught as an engineer and began her career in non-executive positions at NBCUniversal and as a writer for TechCrunch.

She co-founded Zivity, an adult-themed social networking site, and became an early angel investor in companies including Uber, SpaceX, DeepMind, Postmates, Affirm, Niantic, Carta, Thumbtack, Flexport, and Opendoor. In 2016 she became the first woman investing partner at Founders Fund, a venture capital firm co-founded by Peter Thiel. She served as partner until 2024.

She is currently General Partner at Long Journey Ventures, an early-stage VC fund she co-founded with Arielle Zuckerberg and Lee Jacobs. In March 2025, the firm closed its fourth fund at approximately $181.8 million.

She is married to Scott Banister, an entrepreneur and angel investor (co-founder of IronPort, early PayPal investor). She identifies as a socially liberal libertarian and came out as genderqueer in 2016. Based in San Francisco.
