Donor Vetting Protocol (Automated Web Screening)

 

Step 1. Identity Resolution

 

Purpose is to ensure the correct individual or entity is being evaluated.

 

Search queries:

"<Full Name>" biography

"<Full Name>" company OR founder OR investor

"<Full Name>" net worth

"<Full Name>" linkedin

"<Full Name>" wikipedia

Extract:

  • Occupation
  • Affiliated companies
  • Known aliases or name variations
  • Country of residence
  • Public profiles (LinkedIn, Wikipedia, company pages)

 

Flag if multiple individuals share the same name. Resolve using company or location identifiers.

Step 2. Criminal and Legal History

 

Search queries:

"<Full Name>" arrest

"<Full Name>" criminal

"<Full Name>" lawsuit

"<Full Name>" court

"<Full Name>" indictment

"<Full Name>" fraud OR bribery OR corruption

Sources to prioritize:

  • Major news outlets
  • Court record summaries
  • Government enforcement agencies (DOJ, SEC, FTC)

 

Extract:

  • Criminal charges
  • Civil litigation
  • Settlements
  • Regulatory actions

 

Flag if any of the following appear:

  • Fraud
  • Corruption
  • Sexual misconduct
  • Human trafficking
  • Financial crimes

Step 3. Association With Known High Risk Figures

 

Focus on documented relationships with individuals involved in major scandals.

 

Search queries:

"<Full Name>" Epstein

"<Full Name>" "Jeffrey Epstein"

"<Full Name>" "Epstein associate"

"<Full Name>" "Epstein flight logs"

"<Full Name>" "Epstein donations"

Also search associations with:

"<Full Name>" Ghislaine Maxwell

"<Full Name>" trafficking

Flag if:

  • Named in Epstein related reporting
  • Appears in flight logs
  • Documented meetings or financial ties
  • Mentioned in lawsuits involving Epstein

 

Note that some legitimate contacts occurred in professional settings. Human review required if any hits appear.

Step 4. Reputation Risk Screening

 

Search queries:

"<Full Name>" controversy

"<Full Name>" scandal

"<Full Name>" criticism

"<Full Name>" investigation

"<Full Name>" allegations

Extract:

  • Nature of allegations
  • Whether verified by multiple outlets
  • Date of incidents

 

Flag if allegations involve:

  • Sexual misconduct
  • Abuse
  • Human rights violations
  • Financial misconduct

Step 5. Philanthropy and Institutional Relationships

 

Purpose is to contextualize donor activity.

 

Search queries:

"<Full Name>" donation

"<Full Name>" philanthropy

"<Full Name>" foundation

"<Full Name>" university donor

Extract:

  • Major institutions receiving donations
  • Known philanthropic foundations
  • Public controversies around donations

 

Flag if institutions previously returned donations.

Step 6. Political Exposure and Sanctions

 

Search queries:

"<Full Name>" sanctions

"<Full Name>" OFAC

"<Full Name>" politically exposed person

"<Full Name>" lobbying

Sources:

  • Government sanction lists
  • Major investigative journalism

 

Flag if:

  • Sanctioned individual
  • Politically exposed person linked to corruption
  • Foreign influence investigations

Step 7. Summary Risk Scoring

 

For automation, assign simple categorical outputs.

 

Example:

0 = No notable concerns

1 = Minor controversy

2 = Significant allegations or litigation

3 = Criminal conviction or severe reputational risk

Automatic escalation triggers:

  • Any Epstein related result
  • Criminal conviction
  • Multiple independent allegations of serious misconduct

 

Escalated cases should undergo manual review.
