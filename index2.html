Computation & Consciousness

Where Science Meets Consciousness

For three decades, this conference has been the world's premier gathering for researchers, philosophers, clinicians, and technologists exploring the fundamental nature of conscious experience. From neuroscience to quantum physics, from artificial intelligence to contemplative practice, we bridge disciplines to advance understanding of the mind.

From Quantum Biology to Global Awareness

Investigating consciousness at every scale — from microtubule quantum processes within neurons to the emergence of unified subjective experience.

Conference Themes

This year's program spans the full breadth of consciousness studies: neural correlates of consciousness, quantum approaches to the mind, philosophy of mind and the hard problem, AI and machine consciousness, psychedelic science, contemplative traditions, clinical disorders of consciousness, and the origins of sentience in biological evolution.
