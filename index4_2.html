Since 1994

The Original Consciousness Conference

Founded in Tucson, Arizona, this is the world's oldest and longest-running scientific conference on consciousness. For over three decades we have pioneered a uniquely open forum for the study of mind and experience.

Open Inquiry

Where Bold Hypotheses Are Welcome

From parapsychology to panpsychism, from contemplative traditions to AI sentience — we give equal platform to mainstream science and unconventional perspectives. No serious question is off the table.

Quantum Biology

Consciousness at the Quantum Scale

Orchestrated objective reduction, quantum coherence in microtubules, and the Penrose–Hameroff theory continue to spark debate and generate new experimental predictions at the intersection of physics and neuroscience.

Agency & Determinism

Neuroscience, philosophy, and physics converge on the oldest question: do we truly choose, or is volition itself a product of deterministic processes we cannot access?
