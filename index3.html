01 — About

Three Decades of Exploring the Mind

Since 1994, this conference has convened the world's leading researchers in consciousness science. From the neural correlates of awareness to the philosophical puzzles of subjective experience, we foster rigorous cross-disciplinary dialogue that pushes the boundaries of what we know about the mind.

“Consciousness is the one thing that cannot be an illusion.” — David Chalmers
Beyond the Boundary
02 — Themes

Where Every Boundary Is a Starting Point

Our program spans the full spectrum: neural correlates and global workspace theories, quantum biology and orchestrated reduction, psychedelic-assisted research, AI sentience, contemplative neuroscience, parapsychology, the hard problem, and the evolutionary origins of subjective experience.

Philosophy of Mind
03 — Frontier

The Hard Problem Meets New Science

From panpsychism to integrated information theory, from predictive processing to higher-order thought — hear the architects of today's leading theories present new evidence and engage in direct debate. Six days of plenaries, workshops, posters, and experiential sessions.

Quantum Biology
04 — Quantum Scale

Consciousness in the Microtubule

Orchestrated objective reduction, quantum coherence in microtubules, and the Penrose–Hameroff theory continue to generate new experimental predictions at the intersection of physics and neuroscience.
