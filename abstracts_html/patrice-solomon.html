    METAPHOR, the language of connection between conscious realms

    The premise is that all reality is Conscious, and that Conscious fields within nonphysical realities frequently communicate with our physical reality, employing the use of metaphor. Thus, the investigation of connections between physical and nonphysical realms of reality should consider not only direct psi evidence of shared information, but also metaphorical evidence which clearly implies connection. Examples of metaphorical connections will include precognitive dreams with clear relevance to later physical events, as well as pictures created based on internal visuals of remote targets, with a clear metaphorical correspondence. The pictures and the remote target photos can be viewed side by side with the connections explained. Specific instructions will be provided for others to achieve and intuit metaphorical connections with psi experiences. Lastly, research methodological implications of using metaphor in the investigation of psi phenomena will be discussed.

    About the speaker

    Ph.D. in clinical psychology from Syracuse University.  50 years working as a psychologist.  Psychotherapist, evaluator, consultant to the Federal Social Security Disability system.  I once made a poster presentation for this conference in Switzerland, which addressed the application of the scientific method to subjective experiential data without the possibility of replication.  I am not associated with any university position.  I am well read in physics.  I have been learning remote viewing and Mind Sight.
