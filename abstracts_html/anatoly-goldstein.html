    Geometry of Direct Voice Mediumship

    It is shown how multidimensional complex Hilbert space can be used as a convenient geometrical model explaining mechanism of direct voice mediumship. A specific model of experiences described in the biographical book of direct voice medium Leslie Flint "Voices in the Dark" is provided. The key idea is that while vectors of individual human attention are aligned along all kinds of personal and professional interests in everyday life creating a lot of noise preventing spirits from accessing the mind of a medium there are situations when this is not the case. For example, once a movie is started at a movie theater, the attention and intention vectors of most of the spectators are focused on the movie screen. At the same time the directions roughly parallel to the plane of the movie screen are available for possible spirits to access a medium if he or she is present in the movie theater. Under these conditions when spectator personal and professional attention and intention vector noise is minimized, minds of potential spirits can interfere with the mind of the direct voice medium resulting in acoustic signals produced around the medium. A few examples of short sound files recorded on direct voice mediums available online are played back.
