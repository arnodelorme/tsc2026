    The Universal Law of Simultaneity: Direct Recognition and Intuitive Reports in the Awakening Journey

    1) The Universal Law of Simultaneity: Direct Recognition and Intuitive Reports in the Awakening Journey 2) Topic area Experiential approaches, philosophy, enlightenment and awakening, nonduality, consciousness frameworks 3) Presenter Terrence LaMont Bellows, spiritual guide, teacher, practitioner, recognized for intuitive research and living awakening 4) Overview This presentation introduces the Universal Law of Simultaneity, a consciousness framework integrating intuitive research with direct awakening experience Rooted in deep experiential work associated with the School of Metaphysics and the presenter’s own recognition, it proposes that awakening is not a goal, attainment, or path Awakening is presented as immediate and timeless, a direct recognition of consciousness already present Rather than analyzing states or chasing transformation, the session invites participants into direct recognition of what precedes effort, seeking, and self improvement 5) Foundations Simultaneity is expressed through four lived principles, informed by collective intuitive insights and firsthand recognition A) Perception as expression Consciousness energy manifests through every perception, no experience is inherently correct or incorrect, each perspective functions as a complete movement of awareness revealing itself B) Non doing and pure observing The felt sense of personal agency softens in direct experience, awareness is recognized as pure observing, thoughts, sensations, and actions arise within a single unowned field C) Dissolution of concepts Ultimate recognition cannot be captured by concepts, teachings and frameworks serve as provisional supports that naturally dissolve as consciousness recognizes itself D) The pathless path, acausal awakening Enlightenment is recognized as an uncaused non event, the seeking journey is respected as meaningful while also seen as unnecessary in the moment of recognition, revealing the seeker has never left home 6) Methodology The session blends documented intuitive reports, phenomenological reflection, and real time contemplative inquiry Drawing from metaphysical traditions and contemporary nondual insight, it emphasizes direct experience over analysis The format includes brief narrative framing, shared inquiry, and periods of silent recognition so insight can unfold experientially 7) Contribution The Universal Law of Simultaneity bridges intuitive metaphysical research and direct spiritual experience by situating awakening as present, uncaused, and consistently accessible It reframes spiritual practice as recognition within consciousness rather than a means to reach realization This approach supports consciousness researchers, philosophers, and practitioners exploring the interface of intuitive knowing and first person phenomenology 8) Intended impact This session invites interdisciplinary dialogue across academic, experiential, and intuitive approaches within consciousness studies It challenges progress oriented paradigms that equate awakening with effort, transformation, or attainment, presenting simultaneity as the underlying principle unifying inquiry and realization Participants are encouraged to move beyond conceptual understanding into direct encounter with the living field of awareness 9) Keywords Simultaneity, nonduality, intuitive research, awakening, School of Metaphysics, consciousness, pathless path, direct recognition, experiential inquiry, spiritual teaching

    About the speaker

    Sri Terrencelamont (Terrence Lamont Bellows, M.Div.) is an ordained minister, metaphysical teacher, and National Peace Ambassador whose work integrates consciousness teaching, sacred ceremony, and intuitive counsel. Affiliated with the School of Metaphysics and Eden Theological Seminary in St. Louis, Missouri and operating through his media brand We Are Valuable Media, he is the originator of the Universal Law of Simultaneity — a metaphysical framework revealing the infinite as already fully present in every moment of existence. His practice includes cord-cutting ceremonies, intuitive readings, and workshop facilitation through Mystic Valley, as well as community spiritual leadership through the Everlasting Community of the Divine Family. His teaching draws from channeled primary source material, ancient wisdom traditions, and cross-disciplinary research, with a voice shaped equally by rigorous inquiry and lived spiritual experience.
