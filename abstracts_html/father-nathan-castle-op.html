    Navigating Afterlife Consciousness by Helping Souls Cross Over

    This work explores how sudden, violent, and traumatic deaths can leave a soul's consciousness disoriented or temporarily unable to transition. Working under the guidance of the Holy Spirit, Father Nathan has assisted more than 700 souls in shifting their consciousness from states of disorientation in the afterlife to healing and crossing over. Beyond addressing the mechanics of discarnate consciousness, Father Nathan highlights the profound pastoral applications of assisting those in the afterlife to cross over. By dispelling prevailing cultural and theological misconceptions about deceased loved ones being "stuck," Father Nathan's framework provides a comforting paradigm for the living. The presentation shares his work from his three-volume series, Afterlife, Interrupted. He demonstrates how healing in the afterlife consciousness can alleviate agonizing grief, foster deep healing to move on to the next level, and expand human capacity for compassion, connection, and love.

    About the speaker

    Father Nathan Castle, O.P., is a Dominican Friar, author, podcast host, and retreat leader. Over the past 27 years, his unique ministry rooted in the Catholic Church's mystical tradition has helped more than 700 souls transition from one afterlife plane to a more joyful one. Father Nathan believes that providing such help is something the Holy Spirit has given him and his prayer partners to do.

    This work, referred to as The Interrupted Death Experience, is detailed in his latest publication, Afterlife, Interrupted: Book 3. Father Nathan emphasizes the power of these life-altering examples to make us more compassionate, connected, and loving. He dispels misconceptions about deceased loved ones being stuck and provides support for those navigating grief, emphasizing the possibility of moving forward in peace. He is the author of the Afterlife, Interrupted series—books 1, 2, and 3—and And Toto, Too: The Wizard of Oz as a Spiritual Adventure.

    MA and MDiv degrees from the Dominican School of Philosophy and Theology at the Graduate Theological Union in Berkeley, California.

    The Science of Consciousness, Concurrent presenter 2025. International Association of Near Death Studies Conference presenter 2023, 2024, 2025, Spiritual Awakenings International Conference presenter 2024, 2025, 2026.
