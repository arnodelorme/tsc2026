    Eliminating Paradoxes with Paradoxes

    The brain is essentially a 'coordination generator' that maps in real-time the paradox between internal needs and external predictions, and restores system coordination by generating context sensitive responses. Therefore, consciousness is the "coordination" that arises when paradoxes (Decision based on prediction, Decision negates prediction) are represented, processed, and resolved in embodied systems. From a thermodynamic perspective, life exists on negative entropy. The function of the brain is an active process of minimizing entropy, dedicated to compressing noisy data into low entropy internal representations. The sublimation of biological evolution into a logically consistent breakthrough is highly consistent with the definition of consciousness in quantum dialectics. Consciousness is not an accidental byproduct of neural activity, but an emergent synthesis of evolutionary contradictions. The coexistence of prediction and negative prediction is known as the "superposition state of cognition" in quantum cognitive science. Comparing the "superposition state" to the logical state of "coexistence of prediction and negative prediction" before decision-making not only accurately captures the core metaphor of quantum cognitive science, but also directly addresses the dilemma faced by traditional materialism in explaining consciousness. If the brain is in a state of "coexistence of prediction and negative prediction" before making a decision, then the act of "making a decision" is essentially a cognitive collapse. Free will is no longer illusory, but in the ocean of quantum possibilities, consciousness actively points to a certain outcome. This viewpoint not only acknowledges the foundation of physical reality (quantum activity), but also endows consciousness with the initiative to transcend classical mechanical determinism. A 'quantum superposition epistemology': reality (including our cognitive reality) is not an absolute existence independent of the observer, but is dynamically generated through associations, perspective dependencies, and measurements (decisions, actions). When the decision is ultimately executed, this superposition state collapses, creating a definite reality. When you observe an electron, it instantly collapses from a probability superposition state (both wave and not wave, paradoxical state) to a deterministic state (wave or particle, logical state). This has an astonishing mathematical symmetry with the "Decision negates prediction": Prediction is probabilistic and multidimensional (from Is), while decision is exclusive and absolute (Ought to be). This collapse manifests macroscopically as a decisive decision (which negates other possibilities in the progress of prediction), and microscopically as the observed behavior leading to the collapse of the quantum state. The hippocampus and prefrontal cortex collaborate to trigger a mechanism called 'reverse inhibition'. This biochemical signal will instantly cut off synaptic pathways leading to unselected plans, causing those once fiercely contested alternative plans in the brain to become blurred in a very short period of time and even produce the illusion of 'I never thought of choosing it'. So, as' eliminating paradoxes with paradoxes' holds at the quantum level: consciousness terminates the infinite contradictions of the underlying world by forcibly choosing a definite value. Multimodal signals are precisely the patches that are forcibly erased, and through cross validation across dimensions, make this logic collapse appear real and consistent.
