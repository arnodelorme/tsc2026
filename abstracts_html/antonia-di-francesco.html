    EEA Energetic Emission Analysis, measure for consciousness states

    The measuring of awakening conditions and resulting states of incresed consciousness has been investigated by Iomed. Inter alia, states of deep presence, experiencing joy and inner peace, trust, and higher state of consciousness, induces changes in measurable biophysical parameters. The instrument used is the GDV Bio-Well Camera, which detects, elaborate and analyses the emission of biophotons from the fingertips. The shape, size, luminescence, and position of these emissions are not random, but are interconnected to reflected areas on the fingertips in a holographic vision of the Universe and the human body. The reading of an EEA provides a holistic understanding of the predispositions and weaknesses present within the entire human system. Measurements of human energetic emissions were taken through Bio-Well EPI (Electro Photonic Imaging) GDV (Gas Discharge Visualization). Bio-electrography parameters of all fingers of both hands were measured with computerized complex Bio-Well GDV-camera, developed by Konstantin Korotkov, of the University of Saint-Petersburg, Russia. Bio-well GDV Camera is used for determining an EEA characterization for examining human psycho/emotional and physiological state, before and after specific treatments, as elucidated in the following. For example, once a personalized treatment of photobiomodulation (PBM) is administered, we thus proceed with a second EEA detection, in order to assess the body's response to the electromagnetic stimulus of light to which it has been subjected. Specifically the PBM device used is the G4 Neuro-espresso Niraxx inc used for 10 min on forehead 850 nm 40Hz The measurements focused on pre-selected parameters, such as the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis, emotional pressure values, general energy values and the functioning of the corpus callosum in connecting the two hemispheres. The changes resulting from the PBM treatment are measured and analyzed as these parameters (‘stress measuring parameters’) offer a direct measure of the state of connection. To this end, at Iomed medical center we have conducted several studies of this nature, proving how awakening experiences, through various techniques (photobiomodulation, mindfulness, or group therapies such as family constellations), lead to changes in these parameters, and therefore leading us to believe that the use of GDV Camera – Bio-Well technology - is a valid tool for performing effective measurements of the state of awareness. The results relating to the variation of the human energy field following photobiomodulation treatments on the reflex zones of the amygdala and prefrontal lobes, following mindfulness experiences, and following a constellation retreat will be presented.

    My approach is based on the premise that both health and illness emerge from specific states of consciousness; consequently, at our Medical Center, we employ techniques, methodologies, and remedies designed to induce states of quantum coherence and enhance consciousness.

    Together with my team, I conduct both empirical and theoretical scientific inquiries, integrating ancient wisdom, modern scientific discoveries, and non-invasive technologies with an open-minded perspective.

    We conduct educational activities—based primarily on experiential practices and the use of non-invasive technological tools—to foster inner peace, dissolve limiting beliefs, and encourage pro-social behaviors, while remaining steadfast in our commitment to promoting human development potential at both individual and collective levels
