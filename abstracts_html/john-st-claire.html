    We Are the Plasma - Toward a Unified Theory of Conscious Plasmoid Intelligence: From Lambda-Vacuum Cosmology to Embodied Human Consciousness

    Emerging theoretical and observational work suggests plasma, the universe's most abundant state of matter, may be a more fundamental substrate for consciousness than current neuroscience recognizes. This talk synthesizes frameworks in plasma cosmology, bioplasma theory, and quantum-coherent information transfer to propose that consciousness arises not from neural computation alone, but as an intrinsic property of self-organizing, electromagnetically coherent plasma systems; of which biological organisms, including humans, may be one expression. Drawing on lambda-plasma cosmological models extending spacetime into higher-dimensional structure, the presentation outlines how vacuum coupled plasma coherence could enable non-local information transfer and self referential awareness. A central proposal explored is that organized plasma states may host entanglement-like coherence structures analogous to the quantum vibrations proposed within microtubules under the Orch-OR model of consciousness (Hameroff & Penrose), suggesting plasma and biological microtubular systems may represent parallel or nested substrates for quantum-coherent conscious processing rather than unrelated phenomena. Drawing on Russian biophysical research into plasma auras, biophoton emissions, and the electromagnetic organization of living tissue; this framework is extended, proposing the multi-cultural spiritual concepts of “prana, chi, ka, numa, mana, etheric double, light body, etc” may correspond to a literal plasma-based vital force pervading living systems. This synthesis is presented as historical and experimental precedent for a plasma-based theory of consciousness bridging ancient cosmological accounts and contemporary plasma physics. It draws upon the published research on interactive plasma phenomena, non-local information transfer, and the physics of consciousness, as well as publications on ball lightning and anomalous luminosities. Original photographic and video documentation, captured personally, and with research colleagues during field observation, will be shared, along with personally reported direct experiential encounters interpreted within this framework. The talk advances the position that humans may be understood as conscious plasma-based entities organized into biological form, inviting rigorous interdisciplinary engagement with this speculative but internally coherent model, providing compelling evidence of a greater mystery yet to be fully understood.
