    Reality is Consciousness

    Consciousness as Fundamental—Deriving the Physical from the Subjective The "Hard Problem" of consciousness—how subjective feeling arises from insentient matter—remains insoluble under the current materialist paradigm because the fundamental assumption is flawed. We propose a radical inversion of this causality: consciousness is not a product of the brain; rather, the brain and the physical universe are products of consciousness. Drawing on the My Big TOE framework, this presentation establishes consciousness as the fundamental, non-physical substrate of reality. Because consciousness possesses no physical attributes—no mass, charge, or spatial location—it cannot be measured by objective instruments designed to detect physical data. It is the observer, not the observed. We posit that what we perceive as the physical universe is a computed Virtual Reality (VR), a probabilistic data stream generated by a Larger Consciousness System to facilitate the evolution of quality (entropy reduction). In this model, we are not biological bodies that generate a mind; we are Individuated Units of Consciousness (IUOCs) that have "logged on" to a simulation to play the role of human avatars. The brain functions not as a generator of self, but as a constraint—a biological interface rule-set that limits data stream access to define the human experience. This theory resolves the inconsistencies of materialism by defining the physical world as a derivative information system. By accepting consciousness as fundamental, we can logically derive the properties of modern physics (such as quantum uncertainty and relativity) as artifacts of a discrete, finite-resource simulation, finally bridging the explanatory gap between objective science and subjective experience.

    About the speaker

    Thomas Campbell is a physicist and consciousness researcher who has dedicated over 50 years to unifying the worlds of objective science and subjective experience. With a background in applied physics and math, and a career specializing in large-system vulnerability and risk analysis for NASA and the Department of Defense, Campbell approaches the study of consciousness with the precision of a systems engineer.

    Campbell completed his Masters in Physics at Purdue University and went on to the University of Virginia to pursue his PhD in experimental nuclear physics.

    Campbell’s insights are grounded in deep empirical research. As a founding member of the research team at The Monroe Institute, he spent decades exploring the frontiers of human awareness. These explorations led to the publication of My Big TOE, a comprehensive model of reality that challenges the materialist paradigm. Campbell argues that consciousness is fundamental—the primary substrate of existence—while the physical universe is a derivative "virtual reality" simulation. His work provides a logical framework that explains the fundamentals of quantum mechanics and relativity while solving the "Hard Problem" of consciousness. He is a frequent international lecturer on the topics of physics, metaphysics and the consciousness connection.

    He has been a keynote speaker at 2025 Psy Games International, guest speaker at the IANDS online event, and a featured expert on The Joe Rogan Experience, Bialik Breakdown, and New Thinking Allowed with Jeffrey Mishlove Ph D. He was also featured at Telephathy Tapes with Ky Dickens. Campbell most recently presented his work to 2000 people in Switzerland.
