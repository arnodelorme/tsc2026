    Consciousness and Reality

    Consciousness and Reality Roger Penrose says that the quantum theory is wrong, and there is a gap present regarding timing when it comes to the wave function (WF) collapse. And when scientists come to measure the state of the electron spin to predict where the spin is pointing to, they (with 100% certainty) do it correctly. "They can confirm BUT can't ascertain." (the measurement problem). Well, actually Penrose is right in his best in town analysis. I admit, there is no up or down before I measure, but superposition of multiple states is misunderstood. However there's something new in this precariously critical moment that I have to explain in my contribution. My experiential approach indicates that I can ASCERTAIN the electron spin and angular momentum (with certainty except perhaps at the orthogonal state (zero-point or due to a misreading). My "extraordinary ability" enables me to access the Quantum World (QW), and conduct experiments. You will get puzzled if you attend a demonstration proving that motion of any object in the QW matches with that in the Objective World (OW). There are two realities: the classical and the quantum. Consciousness does not create them, but unifies this wonderful universe for the onlookers. It works on it in the dark but, quantum mechanically speaking, enables me to watch what is being shown on the Heavenly Screen (HS) of the brain. This paper deals with two points: 1st. Consciousness and reality, 2nd. The electron property of SPIN and WF collapse. My first provable claim: This universe is REAL. To experiment with an idea, I first see a field full of tiny things like specks of dust usually in a state of motion. I have to look for a wave, (allow to call a WF – mathematical term to you --), to determine one end/ side of it while moving and shaping up in the QW. Something like a radius in a circle appears that I consider one end. Then, the second shows up either on the left or right. Between the two I locate the position of the electron. It's just a third radius. Each radius may appear as two radii (one is the extension of another i.e., a diameter spinning and pointing simultaneously to two directions: either up and down or left and right. That's exactly what I do ascertain. My prediction is considered correct when I successfully locate the position of the particle and determine its angular momentum, simultaneously with certainty. Thus violating Heisenberg's "Uncertainty Principle", and Schrodinger's superposition of the "dead-alive-cat" state that causes the WF collapse into one definite state by a conscious observer. I can describe a playing card (or anything in principle) that is randomly chosen and hidden in a box, e.g. number "7 diamond red" or "9 heart" etc. Are you amazed? If not , then sorry, sir. Consciousness and reality could mean nothing to you. In short, this is my second provable claim: I can, without looking, know and describe a hidden object. (the end)
