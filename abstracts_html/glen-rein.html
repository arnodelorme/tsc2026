Independent lab testing, by BioChroma (USP <61>/<62>), confirmed that the NMA™ was free of E. coli, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Salmonella sp., Staphylococcus aureus and yeast/mold with aerobic plate counts well within normal limits.
