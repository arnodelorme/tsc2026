    Unified fractal consciousness theory (UFCT)

    UNIFIED FRACTAL CONSCIOUSNESS THEORY (UFCT) From Universal Field to Embodied Identity — A Fractal Model of Conscious Individuation The Unified Fractal Consciousness Theory (UFCT) proposes a theoretical and falsifiable model shift in consciousness studies: rather than an emergent property of neural activity, consciousness is posited as an informational structure of reality, expressing itself through fractal dynamics, discrete collapse events, and biological integration processes. UFCT addresses what existing frameworks have failed to unify: the universal field, biological form, individual identity, subjective experience, and the logical laws that make reality possible. The process is framed as resonance-based selection from a Quantum Matrix of possibilities, modulated by genetic heritage (as a chaotic attractor) and refined epigenetically through experience. Core Hypotheses Reality’s informational structure is organized in functional layers: Potential (Quantum Matrix), Archetypal and experiential (Morphic/Informational/Buddhabrot Field), Individual (DNA), Biologically codifiable conscious collapse: each significant act of consciousness produces measurable molecular effects — epigenetic modifications, wavefunction collapse in microtubules, and coherent biophotonic emission.The theory is formalized by a Fractal Equation of Consciousness (Cₜ) describing dynamics by which universal information organizes into experiential trajectories guided by informational attractors (Δₖ) and biologically defined thresholds of significance. Computational iteration of Cₜ admits a Buddhabrot-type fractal visualization, interpretable as a statistical map of possible and realized experiential trajectories. Growth is the progressive reduction of Δₖ — the narrowing of the distance between individual and Universal field. A central proposal is Law Zero: the zinc spark at fertilization — the coherent photonic flash accompanying sperm-egg fusion — reframes fertilization not as a purely biological event, but as the moment where a vibrational-conscious-informational field transitions from non-local bulk to biologically anchored identity.The biological interface of UFCT integrates four converging layers: biophotonic emission as the real-time signal of active Qualia processing; microtubular quantum coherence (consistent with Orch-OR and Fröhlich coherence) as the collapse substrate; epigenetic marking as the physical record of integrated experience; and gamma-band neural oscillations as the signature of coherence between individual and universal fields. A key novel prediction completes the model’s symmetry: photonic silence as the measurable signature of integration. When a Δₖ is resolved, biophoton emission on that specific frequency ceases. Integration is not merely a psychological event; it is a measurable biophysical transition from signal to silence, rendering the full integration cycle empirically accessible. Predictions - Novel Qualia integration will trigger measurable biophoton emission in hippocampal regions. - Chromatin remodeling in limbic structures will correlate with shifts in conscious perception. - Quantum coherence in microtubules will precede or coincide with perceptual transitions. - The biophoton functions as a real-time biomarker of cognitive-emotional integration, signaling the gap between known and yet-to-be-integrated information. - Biophoton emission on previously active frequencies will show measurable spectral reduction following reported experiential integration, confirming photonic silence as the physical signature of Δₖ closure. UFCT offers falsifiable predictions bridging consciousness studies, molecular biology, and quantum theory into a single coherent framework.

    About the speaker

    Federico Bisiacchi

    Author, Researcher, and Integrated Wellness Professional

    I am a multidisciplinary author, independent researcher, and practitioner in the field of integrated wellness and consciousness studies. After an early career in management for multinational corporations, a profound inner awakening reshaped my life and worldview, leading me to explore the deep interplay between consciousness, healing, and the energetic intelligence of the human body.

    Over the past twenty years, I have merged scientific inquiry and experiential practice, integrating biology, quantum physics, anatomy, and awareness-based disciplines.

    I hold an MBA in Marketing (Florence, Italy), a degree in Osteopathy (USA), and a Diploma in Clinical Anatomy (Paris, France).

I propose that DNA is not only a biological code but also a luminous, fractal antenna capable of storing conscious information, emitting biophotons, and modulating our connection to the quantum field.
