    Poster
    Ruminating germane intertwined faces of Consciousness and Humankind

    Consciousness, a fabric of the universe, is a continuum which has been enunciated with sublime thoughts by humankind and articulated in elegant and majestic forms. It has been elaborated for millennia with a keen sense in tune with the laws of science and in harmony with nature that it stands unrivalled. The entire creation is regulated and upheld solitarily by eternal all pervasive energy without which life would cease to exist. However, the manifestation of such a spirit force remains invisible to the human eyes. The scientific community has been contemplating to decode the mystery of consciousness. Seen as complementary the eastern and the western perspectives render a comprehensive understanding of consciousness and its very purpose for existence of humankind. The greatest human adventure is to comprehend the evolution of consciousness and transcend its level to reach the ultimate goal. We are in this life to elevate the soul, liberate the spirit, and arouse our inner consciousness. Human quest wonders Where does consciousness come from? Where does it reside? What happens to it during out of body experience (OBE) and what happens to it post-life? These altered states of consciousness in the realm of realizing ultimate truth can be perceived through Ultra Transcendental Meditation (UTM) with perpetual training within the eyes of the observer and the mind of the seeker only under the guidance of a Living Master. The extra sensory perception (ESP) during UTM provides concrete benchmarks in neuroscience, further substantiated in NOETIC science intersecting with integrated information technology (IIT). Stimulating reverberating sound currents and resonating frequencies that are attained/experienced during UTM in communion with the Spiritual Master unfolds the ultimate reality for the individual seekers. The practice of UTM, facilitates connecting Internet among minds in the region of universal mind just as we communicate physically on social networking sites. Thus, the universal spirit and the individual spirit forces are unified as part of connectomics. Scientists have employed advanced magnetoencephalography (MEG) technique aided by artificial intelligence (AI) on Tibetan monks to decipher internal experiences which lack the impingement of cognitive science in absence of first-person experience. Each of us can manifest the consciousness that transcends space, time, and linear causality. Scientists should also engage themselves in meditation for first-person experiential learning towards unification with higher levels of consciousness converging to the Omni quantum spiritual field. This follows from the fact that there is a minuscule design at each level and a grand design at cosmos level. In order to overcome the sufferings, pain, sorrow prevailing in the world today, the mankind should adapt Dayalbagh way of life for redemption imbibing spirituality, humility and empathy as hallmark of life under a living spiritual master.
