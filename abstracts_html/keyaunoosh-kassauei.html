    From Near-Death Experience to Conscious Evolution: Integrating Expanded Consciousness, Spiritual Intelligence, and Emotional Intelligence

    Near-death experiences (NDEs) offer a unique window into questions at the center of consciousness studies: What is consciousness? Is consciousness entirely dependent upon the brain? What happens to the sense of self when ordinary boundaries of embodiment and identity dissolve? And, perhaps most importantly, what happens to consciousness after the experience? This presentation explores these questions through the phenomenology and long-term integration of my own near-death experience, together with my subsequent work as a physician, researcher, and emotional and spiritual intelligence coach. During my NDE, I experienced a profound state of expanded consciousness characterized by a dissolution of ordinary boundaries between self and other and an experience of interconnectedness that I understood as One Consciousness. The significance of the experience was not limited to the extraordinary state itself; it initiated a continuing transformation of identity, values, purpose, relationships, and understanding of human potential. I propose that NDEs and other spiritually transformative experiences (STEs)—including out-of-body experiences, shared-death experiences, and after-death communications—should be examined not only as anomalous states of consciousness, but also as potentially significant catalysts of human development. The central question shifts from “What happens during an NDE?” to “How does an expanded experience of consciousness transform the person who returns?” That is where we discuss the after effects of NDE and integration to life and leadership . Building on my work in emotional and spiritual intelligence coaching and on a broader research framework concerning spirituality, human flourishing, and conscious evolution, I propose an integrative model in which transformative experiences move through four developmental processes: experience, meaning-making, integration, and embodiment through our PIERS model , Which stands for physician intelligence/PQ, Intellectual intelligence/IQ , Emotional intelligence/EQ, Relational intelligence/RQ and Spiritual intelligence/SQ . Emotional intelligence may provide capacities for regulating and integrating the emotional consequences of transformative experiences, while spiritual intelligence may facilitate meaning, purpose, interconnectedness, transcendence, and the reorganization of identity. Coaching can provide a structured, non-pathologizing environment in which these capacities are translated from extraordinary experience into ordinary life for a better quality and peak performance. This framework does not assume a particular metaphysical explanation for NDEs. Instead, it takes the experiencer's phenomenology seriously while maintaining an open inquiry into competing explanations of consciousness. The reported experience of unity or One Consciousness is therefore presented as a phenomenological finding and a question for further investigation rather than as a predetermined scientific conclusion. I Believe the long-term significance of NDEs may ultimately lie not only in what they reveal about consciousness at the boundary of life and death, but in their potential to transform how individuals understand selfhood, relationship, purpose, compassion, and humanity in relationship to a larger whole. Integrating NDE research with emotional intelligence, spiritual intelligence, and consciousness-based coaching may offer a pathway for translating spiritually transformative experiences into individual and collective conscious evolution.

    About the speaker

    Dr. Keyaunoosh Kassauei, MD, is Founder /Creative Director of My Conscious Coaching Group (MCCG), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit advancing conscious leadership, emotional and spiritual intelligence, and whole-person transformation.

    Deeply inspired by her own near-death experience (NDE), Dr. Kassauei integrates her personal transformative journey with neuroscience, coaching psychology, spirituality, and consciousness studies to explore pathways for healing, meaning, and human flourishing.

    As a physician and executive coach, podcast host, and a Professional Fellow at the Institute of Coaching, McLean/Harvard Medical School, she leads initiatives in spirituality in coaching, healthcare, and leadership. She is also founder and leader of Global and Local Healthcare Professionals for Near-Death Studies ( IANDS).

    Her research focuses on emotional and spiritual intelligence and conscious evolution in soulful healthcare leadership, Human flourishing, moral injury recovery and trauma mitigation in spiritually transformative experiences (STEs) and other transpersonal experiences like NDE, OBE and SDEs.
