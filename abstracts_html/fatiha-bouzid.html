    Poster
    Title: Bouzed Base of Conscious Existence (B + F = Nf): A Unified Transdisciplinary Framework from Neuroscience to Cosmology

    Abstract: Humans came to calculate not to be calculated. This paper presents the Bouzed Base of Conscious Existence (B + F = Nf), established on November 8, 2025, which distinguishes three ontological levels: (B) functional processing instinct, computation, and algorithmic mechanisms shared by humans, animals, and machines, operating in machine time (t); (F) inner sovereignty moral consciousness, veto power, and foundational imagination, operating in human time (τ); and (Nf) existential narrative trace the trace of meaning that swims in both times. The framework rests on a fourfold clinical proof demonstrating that (B) and (F) are functionally separable: sleep paralysis, sleep-related eating disorder, distracted driving, and hearing at end of life (Blundon et al., 2020). Applied to the Kanzi study (bonobo) in Science (2026), the framework distinguishes between need-based imagination (B) and foundational imagination (F). Applied to AI, the framework reveals that machines are pure (B) simulating (F) outputs without conscience, necessitating the structural embedding of human veto power as an ethical safeguard. Applied to cosmology, the golden rule emerges: the problem lies in the detector, not in the universe dark matter as an epistemological shadow. This framework offers a unified conceptual language suitable for all conference tracks through one coherent principle: consciousness is not computation; consciousness is sovereignty. Keywords: consciousness, inner sovereignty, B + F = Nf, veto power, B/F separation, artificial intelligence, dark matter, foundational imagination, transdisciplinary framework, human time

    About the speaker

    The Bouzid Rule of Conscious Existence (B + F = Nf), established on November 8, 2025, distinguishes three ontological levels. B is Functional Processing, operating in machine time: instinct, computation, algorithms shared by humans, animals, and machines. The cosmos is B galaxies spin without knowing they spin. Your heart beats without your permission. F is Inner Sovereignty, operating in human time: moral consciousness, conscience, veto power the capacity to say no to what is functionally correct because it is morally wrong. Nf is the Existential Narrative Trace bridging both times: what remains writing, memory, identity, meaning. Not the immortality of the body, but the immortality of meaning. The human equation is [B ⇄ F] → Nf. Humans came to calculate, not to be calculated.

    This framework offers a third way it neither reduces consciousness to computation nor dissolves it into cosmic unity. It defines consciousness as inner sovereignty an ethically charged witness exercising veto power over both biological drives and algorithmic outputs.

    I present a quadruple clinical proof that B and F are functionally separable. Sleep paralysis: F fully active while motor B is paralyzed. Sleep-related eating disorder: B operates efficiently while F is entirely absent. Distracted driving: B and F compete for neural resources. End-of-life hearing: F persists even as B collapses.

    Applied across TSC tracks: In neuroscience, global network integration is the neural basis of F. In philosophy, the Kanzi bonobo study (Science, 2026) distinguishes need-imagination (B) from foundational imagination (F). In AI ethics, artificial intelligence is pure B simulating F outputs without conscience. Human veto power must be structurally embedded. In cosmology: The problem lies in the detector, not in the universe. Dark matter is an epistemological shadow. The dark matter problem and the hard problem of consciousness are structurally identical both arise from conflating the detector with the detected.

    Why all these disciplines in one framework? Because it is the human rule. Every discipline studies humans from one angle but does not see them whole. This rule began from the whole human being from life, pain, and the question. Everything returns to two things: what functions automatically (B), and what chooses (F). And the trace they leave behind (Nf). Everyone who reads this rule finds themselves within it: the philosopher finds their question, the physician finds their patient, the neuroscientist finds their brain, the believer finds their God, the lover finds their love, the migrant finds their exile.
