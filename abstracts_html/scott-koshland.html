This encodes this reaction “template” into the Neutrino field. This Neutrino entanglement results in the “decoherence” of the reaction at the quantum level forming its products. The entangled Neutrino can act at a distance and retains the entangled memory until the Neutrino physically interacts with matter. The Neutrino field acts as a complex program encoding the biological processes which are the “ Life Force” or “Energy Spirit” that gives life to matter. This “Etheric Body” or “Spirit” is the information that is contained in the Neutrino Field that holds the memory of the complex (quantum) interactions occurring in the body”.
