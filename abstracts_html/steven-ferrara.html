    A New Paradigm on Death

    Death effects most people with deep grieving, intense emotional distress, sadness, and even anger. It makes living life more fearful in most societies. But, when death is approached with compassion and even curiosity, we can look at it from the side of the deceased, and that allows for a new context of understanding. Birth and death are on the same polarity, but Life has no opposite and therefore must be looked at from a non-dualistic view. A non-dualistic model is the new paradigm and takes us beyond thought and emotion to a higher dimension of mind power. As we explore the death of our human based form, and the accompanying fear, thoughts, and emotions, we will come to a place of "what can be" as opposed to "what could have been". As we move forward into this more open-minded willingness, and away from the fear-based resistance of our intellectual mind, we will come upon consciousness that is not dependent on form. In actuality, humanness is dependent, and only has its existence, because of consciousness. We are the awareness of this consciousness, in our essence. We will take a deep dive into awareness of consciousness and how this can completely change how we experience life and view death. This has the effect of living life from the "inside/out" as opposed to the "outside/in". This is the critical point that humanity is moving through at this time. And a new paradigm on death has the potential to accelerate this journey.

    About the speaker

    Steven Ferrara has been a lifelong dedicated student of consciousness studies. For two decades, he was a nationally recognized Instructor for the Concept-Therapy Institute, which offers comprehensive courses on physical, mental, and spiritual education. Steven devoted himself to an additional 20 years of intense inner research documented with over 500,000 words in personal journals, where he explores birth, death, and Life. These journals and the lessons learned are shared in his best-selling book Grief to Gratitude/Finding the Inner Pathway to Healing and Peace and is available on Amazon.

    Steve is also recognized in business, having been the CEO of a mid-size financial services company, and as a member of the Hall of Fame of an International Financial Institution. But he is most proud and dedicated to his 3 children and granddaughter and their growing families. He lives in Newberry Fl, enjoys staying active, riding motorcycles, and engaging in deep and meaningful conversation.
