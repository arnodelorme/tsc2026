    Redefining the Default Mode Network using fMRI Bold sequences and it's role in cognition of 'Self '

    The Default Mode Network has largely been localised to the Prefrontal Medial Frontal Cortex, Angular gyrus and the Posterior Cingulate Cortex. In my study of 22 cases, the extent of the DMN is spread over a larger area involving the visual cortex, the somatosensory cortex which contribute in a significant manner to perceiving the sense of 'Self'. This presentation is part of my thesis work titled Role of Default Mode Network in genesis of consciousness and the Sense of Self. This research is also directed towards investigating the Hard Problem of Consciousness within the framework of Patanjali Yogasutra, that comments on the uniqueness of every subjective experience. The presentation will attempt to highlight the role of fMRI in expanding the role of the Default Mode Network to include 'Vritti' as mentioned in the Indian Philosophical and Knowledge Systems. This will prove to be a novel approach to addressing the Hard Problem of Consciousness.
