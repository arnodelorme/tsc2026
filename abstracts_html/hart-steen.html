    The Neurocosmic Cube: Evidence for an Ancient Consciousness Science Encoded in Architecture, Astronomy, and Neuroanatomy

    What if consciousness is not produced by the brain but accessed through it, and ancient civilizations mapped this relationship with scientific precision? The Neurocosmic Cube Model presents converging evidence from archaeoastronomy, neuroanatomy, and developmental psychology, suggesting that a unified consciousness framework was empirically developed and encoded across multiple transmission systems designed to survive millennia. Twenty-six monuments across five continents demonstrate statistically significant alignment to a six-direction galactic reference frame visible during the summer solstice: Orion (east, galactic anticenter), Ophiuchus (west, galactic center), Ursa Major/Pisces (north-south galactic poles), plus Cygnus mapping the Z-axis. At Göbekli Tepe, summer solstice alignments encode precise galactic coordinates matching the stellar configuration of ~10,950 BCE. This pattern's cross-cultural persistence across 12,000 years exceeds chance probability and suggests deliberate preservation of cosmic orientation correlated with collective consciousness states during Earth's 26,000-year precessional cycle. The same organizational logic appears in the Cube of Space, an ancient practice technology whose 22 positions map to specific cortical regions and cognitive-emotional functions. These symbolic keys operate as a complete anatomical atlas of consciousness architecture, describing archetypal patterns that correspond to measurable neural activity. The framework converges with established developmental models (Kegan, Loevinger, Graves, Porges), suggesting these are not arbitrary symbols but a functional map of vertical development through which awareness descends into fragmentation and re-ascends into integration. The biological mechanism grounding this top-down evolutionary pressure, termed Supernatural Selection, is established in epigenetic research. Rather than environmental pressure alone shaping biology, consciousness directs its own substrate through meaning-making. Narrative and symbol generate specific qualitative states, and the genome distinguishes between eudaimonic wellbeing (meaning, purpose, transcendence) and hedonic wellbeing (pleasure, comfort) at the molecular level, producing discriminably different patterns of gene expression even when subjective emotional states are identical (Cole et al., 2013). The precessional framework predicts that galactic center orientation facilitates parasympathetic-dominant nervous system states enabling integrative eudaimonic awareness, while galactic anticenter orientation promotes sympathetic-dominant states supporting differentiated survival modes. Through sustained ritual exposure, mythological transmission, and architectural experience calibrated to precessional position, ancient cultures could have systematically shaped collective neurobiology across generations via the documented pathway: Story generates Qualia, Qualia shifts Nervous System State, Nervous System State directs Epigenetic Expression, Epigenetic patterns transmit transgenerationally. This transforms the framework from correlational observation into testable biological transmission system with preserved intervention protocols. Falsifiable predictions: (1) Additional archaeoastronomical sites under summer solstice analysis should replicate the six-direction galactic frame at above-chance rates; (2) Neural states corresponding to Cube-described functions should activate predicted cortical networks measurable via fMRI; (3) Practice protocols based on Cube traversal sequences should produce measurable developmental advancement on ego-maturity assessments, with effect sizes correlating to autonomic regulation capacity. If validated, this framework reframes awakening as a navigable developmental process encoded in the stars, preserved in stone, transmitted through story, and executed through biology across the largest time scales humanity has yet recognized. Consciousness accessed rather than produced, mapped rather than mystified, deliberately preserved through a planetary-scale experiment in species development whose methodology we are now positioned to recover and test.

    About the speaker

    Hart Steen is an independent researcher and creator of The Divine Remembering, a comprehensive research project proposing that ancient civilizations encoded a science of consciousness across sacred architecture, astronomical alignments, and symbolic systems. With an academic background in religion and philosophy, Steen's work synthesizes archaeoastronomy, neuroscience, epigenetics, developmental psychology, and comparative mythology into testable frameworks. His research focuses on demonstrating consciousness as the primary substrate of reality, proposing biological mechanisms by which symbolic narrative and qualitative states shape gene expression and transgenerational inheritance. Steen has developed practical applications in organizational development and education, translating insights about consciousness architecture into frameworks for unlocking human potential and fostering coherence across biological, psychological, and social dimensions of development.
