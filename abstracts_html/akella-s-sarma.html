    Physics of Consciousness - A System Engineering Approach

    PHYSICS OF CONSCIOUSNESS - A NON-PHYSICAL SYSTEMS. A System Engineering Study Scientists studied Origin and Creation of Nature and understood that Nature is made of three Systems, namely, Matter, Time & Space and are considered Primary. Before Matter came into Existence, all that was there was Primordial Void. Scientists Studied Matter in detail. Their Study brought out that, Matter primarily consists of Atoms, who's Individual Characteristics and Properties are Distinct and Preserved. They are the Building Blocks of the Nature. Their ingredients Protons, Neutrons and Electrons, are common across Matter. Deeper dwelling into these ingredients brought out that - they have Sub-Atomic Particles and Bosons are Primary. They are also common across ingredients of Atoms. Study of Bosons and its Origin brought out the role of Higgs Field and Higgs Boson in starting evolution and Creation of Atoms from Nothing-ness of Primordial Void. Their Study was about Physical Systems or Energy systems Such detailed study was needed with respect to other 3 Systems in Nature, namely, Space, Time & Primordial Void, which are Non-Physical Systems. They are Zero Energy Systems. But Physicists left it to Physiologists and Psychologists, who Study Physical and Non-Physical Systems under the banner of Brain, Mind and Consciousness. Without Studying Physics of Non-Physical Systems, Physiologists and Psychologists could not bring out the role of Non-Physical Systems in Nature. They needed to do System Engineering Study to develop Interface Control Definitions. That would have exposed them to role of "i "which links Physical and Non-Physical World, like in equation x+iy=c This Article explores the Physics of Non-Physical Systems and brings out the concept of Zero Energy Systems (ZES), defining them as "Systems with Non-Instrumentable Energy," and shows that Space, Time, Knowledge, Consciousness & Primordial Void are ZES. It proposes a Dualistic Model of Energy, bringing out Physical (Instrumentable) and Non-Physical (Non-Instrumentable) domains, and their Convertibility. This framework is used as bridge between Particle Physics & Natural Sciences, particularly in understanding the emergence of Matter and the role of Knowledge, Consciousness and Dynamism, in Nature. Study of Seed transforming into a mighty Tree, coupled with CERN experiments & Livermore (LLNL) Experiments leads to understand that Atoms are coming into being in our neighbourhood every day and we do not need to look at Cosmos. This Article further brings out role the of ZES in Brain. Human Senses receive external inputs in Presentation Format, which is converted into Neural Format, an Energy Guzzling format. Neural Format carries Knowledge, like Radio waves carry Knowledge. Neither Brain, Mind nor Intellect have the ability to Experience the Knowledge in Neural Format. Intellect converts Neural Format to Presentation Format and gives to Consciousness. Presentation Format is also a carrier of Knowledge. Through process of Abstraction, Consciousness experiences that Knowledge. System Engineering Concepts are Used to get results in this study.

    About the speaker

    A S Sarma did graduation in  Electrical Engineering  from Bangalore university India, in 1967.  He was commissioned into Indian Airforce and Trained in Guided Weapons. Worked  in Air Defence Missile Systems and  gained vast Hands on experience field of Missile Guidance Radars, Missile Systems, Instrumentation, Flight tests & Analysis.

    From 1986 Served in DRDL Hyderabad, and Research Centre Imarat Hyderabad  in Design, Development, Integration and testing of  Multi-Disciplinary Systems. As A Multi-Disciplinary System Engineering Expert,  was the Vice-Chairman of Systems Engineering Committee and Design Authority for design and Integration  of Multi-Disciplinary Systems.

    After Retirement, took up Studies of Brain, Mind and Consciousness, & Meditation Practices, again Multi-Disciplinary Subject.
