    Applying Non-Local Conformal Gravity Mathematics to Orch-OR

    Introduction & Problem This paper introduces a speculative synthesis combining Non-Local Conformal Gravity (NLCG) and Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch OR), created by Roger Penrose and Stuart Hameroff. Physicists object to classical Orch OR because general relativity introduces unphysical mathematical singularities (\(1/r\) divergences) at the objective reduction threshold (\(\Delta E_G \sim \hbar/\tau\)). Concurrently, biologists argue that environmental thermal noise destroys quantum states within cellular microtubules too quickly. Mathematical Resolution NLCG math explicitly resolves these criticisms. By replacing the Einstein-Hilbert action with a non-local counterpart utilizing a ghost-free entire function as a form factor, \(f(\Box) = e^{-\Box/M_0^2}\), the modified gravitational potential remains finite as \(r \to 0\). This eliminates subatomic singularities entirely, solving the infinite-energy paradox of wavefunction collapse. Biophysical Bridge & Protection To bridge gravity to biology, the spacetime metric undergoes a microscopic conformal transformation (\(g_{\mu\nu} \to \Omega^2 g_{\mu\nu}\)) coupled to the local mass density of the tubulin lattice. This regularizes the Penrose self-energy integral (\(\Delta E_G\)) using a singularity-free distance metric, allowing a sub-nanometer biological mass to trigger organized reduction within neurobiological timeframes (10–500 ms) without macroscale displacement. Biologically, this scale-invariant metric acts as a topological insulator within the tryptophan rings of tubulin dimers. It suppresses environmental thermal phonon scattering (\(k_{B}T\)), shielding internal dipoles from decoherence. Consequences & NDEs: Consequently, consciousness emerges from a scale-invariant, fractal resonance with the quantum vacuum. During clinical death, the cessation of metabolic pumping causes the conformal factor to decouple from the physical proteins. The quantum information, now bound to the non-local, singularity-free geometry of spacetime, remains coherent, physically explaining Near-Death Experiences (NDEs).
