    Exhibitor Packages

    Present your organization to the world's premier interdisciplinary consciousness conference — five and a half days of plenaries, workshops, and concurrent sessions at Paradise Point Resort & Spa. Both packages include the same exhibit space and benefits; pricing depends on your organization type.

    Illustration — an exhibit table at the conference. Large screen shown is an optional AV rental.

    Non-Profit Exhibitor

    $3,000 USD · full 5½ days
    • One 6-foot exhibit table and two chairs for the full conference (October 11–16)
    • One complimentary conference registration
    • Your organization mentioned in the printed conference program and on the conference website
    • $100 off registration for each additional non-student team member (up to 2 additional)
    Reserved for registered non-profit organizations.

    For-Profit Exhibitor

    $5,000 USD · full 5½ days
    • One 6-foot exhibit table and two chairs for the full conference (October 11–16)
    • One complimentary conference registration
    • Your organization mentioned in the printed conference program and on the conference website
    • $100 off registration for each additional non-student team member (up to 2 additional)

    Please note: a projector or larger screen is not included but may be rented from the conference AV team. Each additional team member must register individually to receive a badge; the complimentary registration covers one person.

    Booth Location

    Exhibit booths are located in the Paradise Foyer — the conference center's main circulation space, directly between the entrance and the Paradise Ballroom where all plenary sessions take place. Attendees pass through the foyer several times a day, including during coffee breaks.

    Only 12 booths are available, and about half are already taken. The exact booth map — showing where each booth is placed in the room — will be available soon. Booths are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.
