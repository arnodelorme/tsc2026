    The Conference Chat Server

    TSC 2026 has a Discord server where participants ask questions, find each other, share rides and rooms, and keep talking after the sessions end. It runs before, during, and after the conference week.

    Join the TSC 2026 Discord →

    Signing up takes about a minute and is free:

    1. Click the invite link above.
    2. If you already use Discord, you are in. If not, pick a username and enter an email address — no app installation is required, it runs in any browser.
    3. Say hello in #general and browse the channels in the left sidebar.
