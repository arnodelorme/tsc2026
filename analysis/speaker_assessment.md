## Field Positioning Legend

- **Mainstream**: Well-established field, publishes in top-tier journals, work widely accepted by peers.
- **Mainstream-to-frontier**: Mainstream credentials and methods, but research conclusions push boundaries (e.g., Levin's "basal cognition", Picard's "mitochondrial psychobiology").
- **Frontier**: Legitimate scientific methods applied to unconventional questions (e.g., biophotons, assembly theory applied to consciousness).
- **Fringe consciousness**: Research on consciousness from a quantum or non-standard framework, limited acceptance outside the consciousness studies community (e.g., Orch OR, QCAT).
- **Fringe / pseudoscience**: Claims rejected by mainstream science; methodological concerns raised by critics (e.g., parapsychology).
