## Detailed Findings

### Stuart Hameroff -- DIRECT

Stuart Hameroff, co-founder and director of the University of Arizona's Center for Consciousness Studies and organizer of the TSC conference, has the most significant Epstein connection among speakers.

**Financial link:** In 2017, Hameroff solicited and received a $50,000 donation from Epstein for the TSC conference. Epstein pledged the money before even knowing the details: "Who are the speakers and what is the cost? I'm in for at least $50,000, before knowing anything." In February 2018, Hameroff again asked Epstein for funding, writing that the conference "could use your generous help again please. Pretty please."

**Personal relationship:** Emails from 2016 show Hameroff and his partner stayed at one of Epstein's New York apartments over a Halloween weekend and spent time at Epstein's home. Multiple Epstein library records reference Hameroff from 2016-2018.

**Shanghai connection:** Investment banker Robert Lawrence Kuhn, an Epstein affiliate, directed Epstein to review a Shanghai Science of Consciousness Program advised by Hameroff. Epstein's charity Gratitude America sponsored the program.

**Institutional cover-up:** The University of Arizona misled the public about the donation, claiming it was "anonymous" when records show the university coordinated and assisted Epstein with making the donation. The FedEx envelope was clearly marked "JEFFREY E. EPSTEIN."

**Consequences:** Hameroff publicly apologized on Feb 3, 2026 and confirmed cancellation of TSC 2026 due to "several speakers and organizers including myself being mentioned in the Epstein files."

### Edward Boyden -- INDIRECT

Edward Boyden, MIT neuroscientist known for optogenetics and expansion microscopy, had repeated contact with Epstein through the MIT Media Lab network.

**Meetings:** The Goodwin Procter investigation (commissioned by the MIT Corporation) determined Boyden met with Epstein on at least five occasions. Two meetings were at Epstein's house, one at a Silicon Valley dinner, and two at a Harvard laboratory.

**Facilitation:** Boyden presented his lab's research to Epstein at the request of former MIT Media Lab Director Joi Ito. Boyden did not accept funding from Epstein, either for his research or for his companies.

**Reid Hoffman dinner (2015):** Epstein referenced a dinner hosted by Reid Hoffman in Palo Alto attended by Zuckerberg, Musk, Thiel, and "MIT neuroscientist Ed Boyden." Epstein described the dinner as "wild."

**Epstein ranch visit:** In a 2013 email, Boyden accepted an offer from Epstein's assistant to visit Epstein's New Mexico ranch with Harvard Professor Martin Nowak.

**Inappropriate exchange:** In 2015 emails between Boyden, Epstein, and Ito, Boyden mentioned being enthusiastic to meet "coaches" that Epstein and Ito mentioned. Epstein subsequently made an inappropriate joke in private with Ito about what kind of "coach" Boyden needed.

**Statement:** Boyden publicly stated he was "uncomfortable at these meetings knowing that Epstein had been convicted of a serious crime" and "deeply regrets attending these meetings."

### Roger Penrose -- INDIRECT

Nobel Prize-winning physicist Roger Penrose had a brief documented interaction with Epstein at a consciousness conference.

**Meeting:** Penrose met Epstein at the June 2017 Science of Consciousness conference in San Diego. Penrose recounted: "I was sitting there talking to Jeffrey Epstein and he was asking me about this institute."

**Funding intermediary:** After the meeting, Penrose relayed Epstein's offer to fund physicist Ivette Fuentes' work on gravitational wave detection. Penrose disclosed Epstein's criminal conviction when asking Fuentes. Fuentes immediately declined on ethical grounds.

**Third Culture network:** Penrose is listed among scientists in John Brockman's *The Third Culture* who had "reported ties to Jeffrey Epstein."

**Context:** Penrose's interaction appears limited to a single conference meeting and his transparent relay of a funding offer (which was declined). This is qualitatively different from sustained relationships like Hameroff's or Boyden's repeated meetings.
