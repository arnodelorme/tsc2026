    Consciousness Conferences

    A guide to upcoming meetings in consciousness science and related fields

    The study of consciousness is an inherently interdisciplinary endeavor, and the community of researchers spans many conferences around the world. Below is a curated list of upcoming meetings relevant to consciousness science, organized chronologically. We encourage participants to engage broadly across these events.

    2026

    • May 21–22, 2026

      International Forum on Consciousness

      Fitchburg, Wisconsin, USA
      Organized by the BTC Institute and Usona Institute, this forum explores “other ways of knowing”—forms of intelligence and awareness that transcend conventional cognition, spanning neurodivergent experiences, animal and plant consciousness, contemplative traditions, and psychedelic states.
    • June 3–6, 2026

      Mind, Brain, and Consciousness Conference (MBCC 2026)

      IIT Mandi, Himachal Pradesh, India
      An interdisciplinary conference integrating Indian knowledge systems with contemporary scientific inquiry across neuroscience, psychology, cognitive science, and AI. Features plenary talks, technical presentations, and panel discussions bridging traditional knowledge and modern science.
    • June 4–6, 2026

      Interdisciplinary Conference on Psychedelic Research (ICPR 2026)

      Philharmonie Haarlem, The Netherlands
      Europe’s premier scientific conference on psychedelic research and therapy. The seventh edition brings together academics, clinicians, therapists, policymakers, and the public for the latest advances in the field.
    • June 30 – July 3, 2026

      ASSC 29 — Association for the Scientific Study of Consciousness

      Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, Santiago, Chile
      The 29th annual meeting of ASSC, the leading academic society for empirical, theoretical, and philosophical investigations into consciousness. Welcomes submissions spanning psychology, medicine, neuroscience, computer science, philosophy, biology, and mathematics.
    • October 1, 2026

      Conference on Consciousness

      UC Berkeley Center for the Science of Psychedelics, California, USA
      A gathering uniting scholars, scientists, artists, and practitioners to explore consciousness from integrated humanities and scientific perspectives, with a focus on altered states and psychedelic experience.
    • October 9–10, 2026

      ALPS — Awareness Lectures on Psychedelics in Switzerland

      Aarau, Switzerland
      The sixth edition of the ALPS conference, featuring two days of cutting-edge psychedelic science, distinguished speakers, interactive panels, and community building in the Swiss tradition.
    • October 9–11, 2026

      The Synapse

      Atlanta, Georgia, USA
      A three-day convening of women leaders working at the intersection of AI, robotics, cognitive science, and consciousness. Organized by Applied Love Labs and led by neuroscientist Julia Mossbridge, the intimate gathering (100 participants) features workshops, facilitated dialogue, and cross-disciplinary collaboration.
    • October 11–16, 2026

      Consciousness Science 2026 (CS 2026)

      Paradise Point Resort & Spa, San Diego, California, USA
      Our conference. Five and a half days of workshops, plenary lectures, concurrent sessions, posters, and interdisciplinary dialogue, bringing together 600–700 participants from around the world to explore the science of consciousness.
      We apologize for the date overlap with MoC7 in Copenhagen and other October events. Our dates were determined by venue availability at Paradise Point, which was contracted well in advance. We look forward to coordination and cross-pollination between the meetings.
    • October 12–16, 2026

      Models of Consciousness 7 (MoC7)

      University of Copenhagen, Denmark
      The seventh biennial conference organized by the Association for Mathematical Consciousness Science. Focuses on philosophical foundations, methodologies and measures, AI and consciousness, and phenomenological investigations. Features Nobel Laureate John O’Keefe and an excursion to the Louisiana Museum of Modern Art.
    • October 14–15, 2026

      Conference on Consciousness and Psychedelics

      UC Berkeley, California, USA
      A two-day event from the Berkeley Center for the Science of Psychedelics exploring consciousness from integrated humanities and scientific perspectives, examining philosophical, artistic, and cultural viewpoints on altered states of consciousness.
