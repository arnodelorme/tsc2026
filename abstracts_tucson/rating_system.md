# Speaker Academic Rating System

## Components (0-100 scale)

### 1. Institution Tier (0-25 points)
- R1 major research university: 25
- R2 accredited university: 18
- Research institute (e.g., IONS, Max Planck): 15
- Industry/corporate research: 12
- Independent researcher: 5

### 2. Publication Count (0-25 points)
- 200+ publications: 25
- 100-199: 22
- 50-99: 18
- 20-49: 14
- 10-19: 10
- 5-9: 6
- 1-4: 3
- 0 or unknown: 0

### 3. Citation Count (0-30 points)
- 20000+ citations: 30
- 10000-19999: 27
- 5000-9999: 23
- 2000-4999: 19
- 1000-1999: 15
- 500-999: 11
- 100-499: 7
- 1-99: 3
- 0 or unknown: 0

### 4. H-index (0-20 points)
- 60+: 20
- 40-59: 17
- 25-39: 14
- 15-24: 11
- 10-14: 8
- 5-9: 5
- 1-4: 2
- 0 or unknown: 0

## Rating Tiers
- 80-100: Tier 1 (Top academic)
- 60-79: Tier 2 (Strong academic)
- 40-59: Tier 3 (Established researcher)
- 20-39: Tier 4 (Emerging/niche researcher)
- 0-19: Tier 5 (Early career/independent)

## Notes
- Fringe science speakers are rated by the same academic criteria
- Community standing within their field is noted but does not alter the score
- Plenary speakers get a "plenary" tag but no score boost
