* **Financial and Cost Concerns:** The most frequent factor influencing attendance is the cost, including high registration fees (especially for students, e.g., $495), expensive hotel/room rates at the Paradise Point Resort (often cited as cost-prohibitive, sometimes over $300/night or $1000 minimum total), and the overall expense of international travel and airfare from Europe, Australia, India, and Canada. Many respondents noted the lack of institutional funding or being on a fixed income.
  * **Scheduling Conflicts and Timing:** Many potential attendees have conflicts because the new October dates overlap with other consciousness-related conferences (particularly the Models of Consciousness Conference in Copenhagen), teaching duties, or prior commitments. Some prefer a later date, like late November/early December, or the original spring timing.
  * **Concerns about the United States:** A significant number of international respondents expressed hesitation or refusal to travel to the US due to the current political climate, perceived instability (referencing the Trump administration), concerns about safety, difficulties with visa/border crossing, and US government data regulations. Several suggested moving the meeting outside the US.
  * **Need for Program Details and Transparency:** Many are undecided until they receive more information, specifically requesting the agenda, list of speakers, details on the 'broader inclusion' initiative, and clarity on whether previously accepted talks/posters are still valid. There is a strong, repeated demand for a detailed, transparent explanation regarding the cancellation of the original Tucson conference and how the organizers are addressing connections to the Epstein files and related ethical issues (including concerns about previous keynote speakers).
  * **Location Preference and Accessibility:** While San Diego is described as an attractive location, and is highly convenient for local attendees (e.g., those in San Diego, Los Angeles, or California), many who live in Tucson or Arizona prefer the original Tucson venue due to ease of travel and lower costs. The availability of an online/virtual attendance option is widely requested due to high travel costs and distance.
