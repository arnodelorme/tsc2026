Since 1994

The Original Consciousness Conference

Founded in 1994 in Tucson, Arizona, this is the world's oldest and longest-running conference devoted to the scientific study of consciousness. For over three decades, we have pioneered a uniquely open forum where neuroscientists, physicists, philosophers, clinicians, and contemplative practitioners meet as equals — united by the conviction that the hardest questions deserve the broadest inquiry.

From Quantum Biology to Global Awareness

Investigating consciousness at every scale — from microtubule quantum processes within neurons to the emergence of unified subjective experience.

Open Inquiry

A Tradition of Intellectual Courage

From its founding, this conference has welcomed bold hypotheses alongside mainstream science. We believe that genuine progress requires open dialogue across every perspective — from integrated information theory to panpsychism, from psychedelic phenomenology to parapsychology, from AI sentience to contemplative insight. No serious question is off the table.
