About the Conference

Three Decades at the Frontier of Mind

Since 1994, this conference has convened the world's leading researchers in consciousness science. From the neural correlates of awareness to the deepest philosophical puzzles of subjective experience, we foster rigorous cross-disciplinary dialogue.

“The study of consciousness is the last great frontier of human knowledge.” — Stuart Hameroff
